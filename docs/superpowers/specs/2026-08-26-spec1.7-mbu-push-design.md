# Spec 1.7 - The MBU push

Status: approved design, 2026-08-26. Follows spec 1.5 (performance ladder,
closed short) and the spec-1.6 stage-0 measurement series (attention probe,
instrument upgrade, int4 lm_head landed). Standing official rows
(docs/BENCHMARKS, record grade, idle box): Vishva007 **27.52 t/s** (72.5%
MBU), RTN/tuned int4-lm_head **30.04 t/s** (69.6% MBU). This spec attacks
the efficiency gap itself: same checkpoints, same correctness bars, more of
the device's measured 590 GB/s turned into tokens.

## 1. Where the deficit lives (all measured; docs/15, docs/12, Task C)

Against the 31.75 ms/token-era vLLM software (now historical - the bar's
container was rebuilt dev365→dev514 under the same tag; a fresh serving
baseline is the operator's), our Vishva step decomposes:

| bucket | ours (ms) | vLLM-implied (ms) | delta |
|---|---|---|---|
| GEMV family (int4 + bf16) | 29.0 | ~28.4 | ~0.6 |
| attention family | 3.84 | \} | \} |
| gdn_step | 0.75 | \} | \} |
| prep + a‖b + boundary | 0.76 | \} ~3.4 total | \} ~3.3 |
| dispatch gap + host | ~0.6 | \} | \} |

Two structural facts orient everything:

1. **The non-GEMV side dominates the deficit** (~3.3 of ~4.6 ms), led by
   attention - NOT the GEMMs.
2. **The machine runs out of load *messages*, not bytes.** The spec-1.6
   probe's hoist-proof control put 39.2% of `attn_decode` in 32-byte
   load-message issue (16.2% cache service). The GEMV shapes stuck at
   533-559 GB/s (90-95% of device) are prime suspects for the same wall.
   vLLM reaching 83% *end-to-end* on this silicon proves ≥10 MBU points of
   our deficit are our overheads, not the hardware's.

**Verified premise (2026-08-26, ocloc on the box):** the Xe2 2D block-load
builtins (`intel_sub_group_2d_block_read_*`) compile in our AOT toolchain
for BMG - the message-count lever is available to OpenCL C kernels today.

## 2. Success bar and stopping rule

- **Success:** ≥ **82% MBU** on the running checkpoint at record grade -
  i.e. ≥ 31.5 t/s Vishva-class and ≥ ~33 t/s on the int4-lm_head
  checkpoints - matching the efficiency vLLM demonstrated on this silicon.
- **The 90%-of-roofline question** (the operator's fold criterion) is
  explicitly re-examined at the gate with the Stage-0-priced ceiling: the
  probes say whether 90% is physically available; the memo carries the
  answer either way.
- **Stopping rule** (as ruled in 1.5): probe-ranked ladder; a lever that
  misses its bar is reverted and recorded as a priced miss; when the ladder
  is exhausted, the gate runs; short → a re-assessment memo to the
  operator; nothing beyond the memo starts without their decision.
- **Correctness bars (unchanged, hard):** the tie-aware golden gate
  (determined rows 100% exact; tie rows set-membership; teacher-forced
  tails) runs BEFORE and AFTER every lever on the lever's checkpoint; full
  suite green; determinism discipline (no fp atomics, fixed grids and
  reduction orders, byte-identical replay); per-op bf16 RNE where the
  family is bit-exact today, the ruled ulp bars where it is not.

## 3. Stage 0 - probes before levers (pre-registered predictions mandatory)

**P1 - wide/2D-block load GEMV probe.** Extend `tools/probe/probe_gemv`
with load-path variants at the six production shapes: (a) widened vector
loads (128-bit per lane), (b) 2D block loads via the verified builtins,
(c) LSC prefetch (left confounded by the attention probe - control for
register pressure this time). Deliverable: a per-shape GB/s matrix beside
the standing 533-584 one, with message-count arithmetic per variant, and a
named verdict: is the 533-559 plateau message-issue (fixable) or silicon
(not)? Pre-register per-variant predictions from the message-count model
before each run; the falsification protocol is the house method precisely
because five models have died this way.

**P2 - comparative probe: vLLM's int4 GEMV at our shapes.** In the
operator's container, time the wNa16 int4 matmul path at the six
production `{K,N}` shapes, batch 1 (torch/IPEX-level microbench; document
the method and its apples-to-apples limits - dequant scheme, layout,
warm-up). Deliverable: their per-shape GB/s beside ours. This settles
"skill or silicon" from the other side.

**P3 - attention message-path transfer check.** If P1's winning load
technique exists, a probe_attn variant applies it to the KV walk
(the measured 39.2% message-issue share). Cheap because probe_attn and its
harness exist.

Stage 0 output: a docs/15 addendum (§1.7 anatomy) with the re-ranked
ladder, each lever priced from probe data, and the 90%-ceiling arithmetic
re-run with the measured plateaus.

## 4. Stage 1 - the ladder (initial ranking; Stage 0 re-ranks)

**L1 - GEMV load-path rebuild** on the plateau shapes (out/o_proj, down,
gate‖up first - they carry the most bytes below peak). Expected yield if
P1 fires: **~1.5-2 ms/token** (533-559 → ~580 GB/s class). Layout
implications are allowed but priced: if 2D block loads want a different
tile geometry, the repack + loader change rides in the lever with its own
unit bars and the gate as arbiter.

**L2 - attention:** the held ~0.5 ms tuning set (`kvt`, `sgtree`
arithmetic-free; `vec2` reassociating - gate arbitrates) plus P3's
transferred load technique if it fires. The lever that reorders softmax
accumulation runs the gate before/after with the standing tap-drift
warning in its brief.

**L3 - fusion tier 1:** RMSNorm→GEMV prologue (retires the `prep_res_fold/
finish` family remnant ~0.48 ms and its 258 launches) and SiLU·mul into
the GateUp epilogue (~0.13 ms + launches). Launch count changes: the
ripple rule binds (every pinned count/module site enumerated in the plan).
Dispatch+ramp savings ride along (~0.5 ms class total with L3).

**L4 - overlap probe (probe-only by default):** second L0 queue / split
lists to hide ramp-drain. Becomes a lever only if the probe shows
≥ 0.3 ms AND byte-identical replay survives; otherwise memo material.

Per-lever acceptance (identical to 1.5's, plus the tie-aware gate): suite
green; gate before/after on the lever's checkpoint; `--profile --repeats`
attribution (floor 0.051/0.011 ms as measured); proxy run; accepted →
record-grade median-of-3 row (sha-named, idle-box-proven) + docs/12
mechanism in the same commit; miss → revert + priced record.

## 5. The gate

Record-grade medians on the idle box for the affected checkpoints.
- **≥ 82% MBU (≥ ~31.5 t/s Vishva-class):** record, update README ladder +
  docs/05 verdict, tag `spec1.7-done`, close.
- **Short:** re-assessment memo per the 1.5 pattern - per-lever yields
  (accepted AND reverted), the remaining gap, the re-examined 90% ceiling,
  and the redesign menu (deeper fusion, layout generation, overlap,
  bucketed alternatives) priced from Stage 0. Memo to the operator; STOP.

## 6. Records

docs/12 probe matrix gains the P1/P2 columns; docs/15 gains §1.7 (anatomy
addendum, ladder, per-lever attributions); docs/BENCHMARKS one row per
accepted lever + the gate rows; docs/07 items updated where probes answer
them; the memo (short path) lands beside 1.5's.

## 7. Constraints (binding; inherited unless stated)

- Tie-aware golden gate hard, before AND after every lever; suite green;
  determinism discipline; RNE/ulp bars per family; `-cl-denorms-are-zero`
  forbidden (build fatals); correctly-rounded div/sqrt default.
- Launch-count/module-count ripple rule: all pinned sites move in the same
  commit (the plan enumerates them; L3 changes both).
- `ProfileEvents::kProfileCapacity` 1024 - verify against any new count.
- Record-grade rows only from a provably idle box (DRM-fd evidence);
  every number labelled measured vs estimated + box conditions + grade;
  no two values for one quantity without a labelled reconciliation.
- C++17 `-Wall -Wextra -Werror`; box workflow `tools/box.sh` (JOBS=44,
  both mtime traps); work on `main`; nothing pushed; tags local.
- The operator's container is used read-only for P2 (no installs).

## 8. Out of scope

MTP / speculative decode; prefill, tokenizer, HTTP (spec 2); prefix
caching; new quantization formats (MXFP4 et al.); lm-eval / model quality;
the vLLM serving re-baseline (operator's); multi-GPU.

## 9. Risks, stated

- **2D block loads may demand a tile geometry our layout-1 doesn't have**
  - then L1's price includes a repack path and a golden re-proof; the probe
  prices this before the lever is cut (P1 runs on synthetic tiles first).
- **P2 may be apples-to-oranges** (different dequant granularity, layout,
  JIT warm-up) - the method section carries the caveats; its role is
  bounding, not precision.
- **Fusion reopens the most-verified kernels** - the tie-aware gate and
  the per-family reference bars are the arbiters; that is exactly what
  they are for.
- **Overlap threatens determinism** - probe-only by default; the replay
  bitwise bar is non-negotiable.
- **The instrument floor** (0.051/0.011 ms, ±0.15 lever spread from
  Task C) bounds what any single lever claim can assert - attribution via
  `--repeats`, record rows for absolutes, the `--profile` lever-spread
  settler runs in Stage 0 alongside P1 (minutes, closes Task C's open
  reconciliation).
