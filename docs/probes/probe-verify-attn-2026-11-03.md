## §3 Numerics

- **N2a (property R):** 100 / 100 trials bitwise at every (KV form, depth, arm) - a row's output does not depend on
  its slot or on its neighbours.
- **N2b / rep:** every DPAS row against v2's M = 1 step at pos + m equals its N1 (v2's M = 1 is the reference's
  rounding); two runs bitwise at every shape.
- **N1, bf16:** worst 0.9999937 (M = 7 / 8 at 512) - 0.9999991; v2 itself is 1.0000000 against the reference.
- **N1, int8:** worst 0.9999932 - 0.9999946 at 512 / 1448 / 11585 / 32768 / 130816, but **0.99998797 at 4096 at
  (q head 19, row 0) for every M and every arm** - below the 0.99999 bar. `pf_flash_attn_kv8` (the prefill's
  production kernel, same P-in-bf16 form) measures 0.99998792 at the same row. It is the P rounding of one peaked row,
  not a lever: every DPAS arm lands on the same value. **Operator ruling 2026-11-03: accepted.** The int8 N1 bar is
  "no further from the fp64 reference than pf_flash_attn_kv8 on the same row" (0.99998792 there; 0.99999 holds at
  every other int8 depth), so int8 4096 is a timing verdict: dpas at M = 3 / 4 (1.16 / 1.58), v2 at M = 2 (0.99) -
  int8 M = 3 / 4 bind DPAS from 4096 keys. `attn_verify_dpas_test` part D states that bar on that row.
